## Notes for Outreach Tool Setup (Apollo / Lemlist / Instantly)

- **Sending domain:** Use `founders@intervue.singularraritylabs.com` or a warmed-up alias
- **Daily send limit:** Max 50/day per inbox to protect deliverability
- **Unsubscribe:** Required by law — add an unsubscribe footer
- **Personalization:** At minimum, use `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}`
- **Timing:** Send Tue–Thu, 9–11am IST for highest open rates
- **Tracking:** Enable open tracking but disable link tracking (less spammy)
