## Feedback Interview Guide (for the 20-min call)

Use this when you get on the call with a PM:

### Warm-up (3 min)
- What's your hiring process like right now for [role]?
- What's the most broken part?

### Demo (5 min)
- Show the 2-minute video
- Walk through one live template creation

### Probing (10 min)
1. "Would you trust this scorecard for a final-round decision? Why/why not?"
2. "What's missing from the candidate experience?"
3. "What would it take for you to recommend this to your Head of Talent?"
4. "What's the one feature that would make this a no-brainer?"
5. "What other tools in your hiring stack does this need to integrate with?"

### Close (2 min)
- "Would you use this for a real open role in the next 30 days?"
- If yes → onboard them to alpha
- If no → ask why and take notes
